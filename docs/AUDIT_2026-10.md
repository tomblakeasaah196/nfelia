# Nfelia audit, October 2026

Date: 18 October 2026. Code at `main` 832b9c8 (after PR #18).

This is a fresh audit of the whole app: money, stock, the shop websites, security, billing, tasks and
reminders, and data integrity. It replaces nothing in `docs/AUDIT_REPORT.md` (June 2026), which covered
an older codebase.

**The short version:**
- Several everyday actions are **broken today** and fail every time:
  - saving an expense;
  - making a delivery note by hand;
  - a walk-in sale;
  - recording a debt settlement.
- Two leaks let one business **see another business's data or customers' signatures**.
- Billing can be **bypassed in three ways**.
- Profit is **wrong**: the P&L only counts receipts, so most invoice payments are missing.

The fixes are grouped into three PRs at the end.

## How to read this

Each finding has an id (M money, S stock and orders, X security, B billing, P tasks and reminders,
W shop websites, D data integrity), a severity and a status:

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Critical | Wrong money or exposed data for real users today, or a way to use Nfelia without paying |
| High | A main feature broken, or wrong results that owners will act on |
| Medium | Wrong in a common but narrower case, or a hardening gap with a real path |
| Low | Edge cases, polish, defence in depth |

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified live | Reproduced against a local copy of the app (MariaDB 10.11, PHP 8.3, demo data) |
| Verified in code | Traced end to end in the code and checked against the production schema |
| Reviewed | Traced by a reviewer; plausible, not re-traced line by line |

## M · Money

**M1 · Critical · Invoices accept another business's client and bank account** · Verified live
- Where: `InvoiceController.php:724-725` (no ownership check on `customer_id`, `bank_account_id`);
  `QuotationController.php:583-596` (the check exists but runs after `$errors` was already tested at :570);
  `ReceiptController.php:334`; `DebtController.php:423`; `DeliveryController.php:410-411`; reads by id
  alone at `InvoiceController.php:187,567`, `QuotationController.php:179`, `ReceiptController.php:207,460`,
  `DebtController.php:100-104`, `PublicController.php:253-264`.
- What happens: a signed-in owner posts another business's `customer_id` and `bank_account_id` (ids are
  sequential). In a local test, Ivie Luxe Hair saved an invoice to Kemi Events' client and bank account and
  then read the client's name, company and phone in the app, and Kemi's account number on the public invoice
  page. Sending, paying or cancelling it also changes the other business's customer totals.
- Fix: check every posted `customer_id`, `bank_account_id`, `invoice_id` and `product_id` belongs to the
  business on save; add `business_id` to every customer, bank and invoice read and update.

**M2 · Critical · Profit and loss leaves out most money received** · Verified in code
- Where: `ReportController.php:40-47` (revenue = receipts only), `:108-112`, `:173-175`;
  `InvoiceController::record_payment` creates no receipt; `DashboardController.php:41-45` uses payments.
- What happens: a ₦50,000 payment recorded on an invoice is missing from the P&L until someone also makes a
  receipt for it. The dashboard counts it, the P&L doesn't, so the two disagree. Deleting a receipt removes
  revenue that was really received.
- Fix: one basis everywhere: payments by `payment_date`, plus receipts with no invoice (direct sales).

**M3 · High · Rounding leaves fully paid invoices stuck at "partially paid"** · Verified in code (reproduced in PHP)
- Where: `InvoiceController.php:404-410`; same pattern `DebtController.php:275-277`.
- What happens: total ₦11,313.87 paid as ₦5,801.30 + ₦5,512.57 leaves a balance of 0.0000000000018, so the
  status stays `partially_paid` with ₦0.00 due. Further payments are refused, and after the due date it goes
  overdue with a ₦0.00 debt and alert. About 1 in 9 two-part payments with kobo hit this.
- Fix: round to 2 decimals before comparing (or work in kobo).

**M4 · Critical · No expense can be saved, and stock purchases can't restock** · Verified in code
- Where: `ExpenseController.php:441` (opens a transaction), `:486` (`generate_doc_number(..., 'EXP')`);
  `functions.php:19-24` (EXP not allowed); `document_counters.doc_type` ENUM has no EXP; restock SQL uses
  `products.stock_quantity` (`:271, :465, :520`; the column is `stock_qty`); movement type `'restock'` is
  not in the ENUM; required columns left out (`:275-278`, `:528-531`); `search_products` (`:353`) selects
  columns that don't exist, so the product picker returns a 500.
- What happens: every new expense fails with "Failed to save expense". Profit is overstated because spending
  since this broke is missing.
- Fix: make `generate_doc_number` join the caller's transaction; add EXP (code + migration); use
  `stock_qty`, `stock_in`, all required columns and row locks; fix the picker's columns.

**M5 · High · Walk-in sales (receipts with no customer) always fail** · Verified in code
- Where: `ReceiptController.php:334` (null customer), `:394-407`; `receipts.customer_id` is `NOT NULL`.
- What happens: the receipt form defaults to "Walk-in Customer"; saving it fails and the sale and its stock
  movement are lost.
- Fix: make `receipts.customer_id` nullable (migration).

**M6 · High · "Record settlement" on a debt never saves** · Verified in code
- Where: `views/debts/view.php:213-221` (no CSRF token, no `X-Requested-With`); `DebtController.php:235-249`.
- What happens: the request takes the non-AJAX path, fails the CSRF check, the page can't read the response
  and shows "Network error". Once that is fixed: an empty method is stored as NULL in a NOT NULL column,
  `card`/`cheque` aren't in the ENUM, invoice debts get no payment row (missing from reports) and the
  invoice's balance doesn't move, so the same money can be recorded again on the invoice.
- Fix: send the token and header and verify CSRF on both paths; limit methods to the ENUM; settle invoice
  debts through the invoice's payment logic.

**M7 · High · Approving a shop order twice makes two of everything** · Verified in code
- Where: `OrderController.php:208-216` (unlocked read), `:233-239` (no transaction), `:280-284`.
- What happens: a double tap or a retry after an error creates two invoices, two receipts, two deliveries,
  two stock deductions. Paid orders also get no `payments` row, so the dashboard misses them, and customer
  totals are never added.
- Fix: one transaction that claims the order first (`... AND status IN (pending, pending_verification)`,
  check the row count); add the payment row and customer totals.

**M8 · High · A quote can be turned into an invoice twice** · Reviewed
- Where: `PublicController.php:109,131,160` (client actions with no status guard); `QuotationController.php:405,466`.
- What happens: after conversion, the client's old open page can still sign (status back to `accepted`) or
  comment (`in_negotiation`), and the Convert button comes back; two converts at once both succeed.
- Fix: allow client actions only on `sent`, `in_negotiation`, `pending_signature`; claim the quote with a
  guarded update before converting.

**M9 · High · Two payments at once can overpay an invoice** · Reviewed (needs concurrency)
- Where: `InvoiceController.php:353,382,404-410`.
- What happens: two people record ₦30,000 on a ₦50,000 invoice at the same time; both pass, `amount_paid` ends
  at ₦30,000 with ₦60,000 of payments, and more payments are still accepted.
- Fix: lock the invoice row, re-check the balance, add with `amount_paid = amount_paid + ?`.

**M10 · High · Customer totals drift (owed, invoiced, received)** · Verified in code
- Where: editing a sent invoice (`InvoiceController.php:808-821`, also allows a new customer); order invoices
  never added (`OrderController.php:454-475`); debt write-off and manual-debt delete (`DebtController.php:343,375`);
  receipt delete (`ReceiptController.php:253-269`); purge (`PurgeController.php:563-591`); cancelling a paid
  invoice; double settlements (M6); double submits (M9, M11); cross-tenant ids (M1).
- What happens: "Clients owe you" and each client's balance stop matching their invoices. `GREATEST(0, …)`
  hides the negative cases.
- Fix: stop storing these running totals by hand: recompute a customer's figures from their invoices,
  payments and direct receipts after every money change.

**M11 · Medium · Double tap on Send or Cancel moves stock and totals twice** · Verified in code
- Where: `InvoiceController.php:282-294` (send), `:481-541` (cancel).
- Fix: guarded status updates (`… AND status = 'draft'`, check the row count).

**M12 · Medium · A written-off overdue debt comes back; duplicate debts appear** · Reviewed
- Where: `InvoiceController.php:41,968`; `DebtController.php:483-485`.
- Fix: one sync that skips invoices that already have any debt row; unique key per invoice debt.

**M13 · Medium · Back-to-back partial payments on an overdue invoice don't reduce its debt** · Reviewed
- Where: `InvoiceController.php:421-423,1012-1017`.
- Fix: always update an open debt from the invoice's new balance.

**M14 · Medium · Cancelling a paid invoice keeps the money in reports but takes it off the customer** · Reviewed
- Where: `InvoiceController.php:484,495,526-537`.
- Fix: block cancelling an invoice with payments (record a refund first), or record the refund.

**M15 · Medium · Receipts made from payments show the wrong balance; a double tap doubles revenue** · Reviewed
- Where: `ReceiptController.php:111,122-130,140` (a GET creates the receipt).
- Fix: compute the balance from the payments up to that one; unique key on `receipts.payment_id`.

**M16 · Medium · Deleting a receipt reverses nothing** · Verified in code
- Where: `ReceiptController.php:253-269`.
- What happens: a deleted direct sale keeps its stock off and the customer's "received"; an invoice receipt's
  revenue disappears while the invoice stays paid.
- Fix: allow deleting only direct-sale receipts, and put stock and totals back when you do.

**M17 · Medium · "If you collect everything" counts overdue invoices twice** · Reviewed
- Where: `ReportController.php:75-102`.
- Fix: add only receivables that didn't come from an invoice.

**M18 · Medium · A discount bigger than the subtotal (or a negative tax) makes negative invoices** · Verified in code
- Where: `InvoiceController.php:728-731,791-793`; `QuotationController.php:552`.
- Fix: clamp discount to 0–subtotal and tax to 0–100, as quotes already do for discounts.

**M19 · Medium · Expenses and their payable debts never agree** · Reviewed
- Where: `DebtController.php:509-537,292-300`; `ExpenseController.php:255-306`.
- Fix: keep the expense and its payable debt in step both ways.

**M20 · Low · Line totals aren't rounded before summing** (`InvoiceController.php:769-778`, `QuotationController.php:605-614`).

**M21 · Low · Booking deposits never reach the books** (`BookingController.php:82-120`): confirming a booking
with a checked deposit records no money.

**M22 · Low · Two doc-number generators share one counter** (stored procedure in invoices, quotes and receipts;
PHP helper elsewhere). Use the PHP one everywhere once it is transaction-aware.

**M23 · Low · ₦ and the 234 phone prefix are hard-coded** (`InvoiceController.php:383,432-433,993,576`,
`CustomerController.php:241`, `DebtController.php:215`), so a Cameroon business sees ₦.

**M24 · Medium · PHP's clock isn't set to Lagos** · Verified in code
- No `date_default_timezone_set` anywhere; the server runs at +02:00. Dates default from PHP (`date('Y-m-d')`)
  in forms and controllers, so sales after 23:00 Lagos time on the last day of a month land in the next month.
- Fix: set `Africa/Lagos` once at bootstrap.

## S · Stock, orders, deliveries and bookings

**S1 · Critical · Delivery signatures are public at guessable addresses** · Verified in code
- Where: `PublicController.php:356-375` (`$delivery["id"] . "_signoff.png"` under `uploads/`); no rule
  protects `uploads/`.
- What happens: `/uploads/delivery_signoffs/1_signoff.png`, `2_…` and so on return every customer's
  handwritten signature, for every business. The posted image isn't checked or size-capped either.
- Fix: random file names under `storage/`, served only through the token-checked delivery page; check the
  image and cap its size; move and rename the existing files in a migration.

**S2 · High · Cancelling or editing an invoice can put back stock that never left** · Verified in code
- Where: `InvoiceController.php:495-523` (cancel), `stock_lines()`.
- What happens: shop-order invoices approved before PR #17 never took stock off, but cancelling one adds it
  back. Changing a product between service and goods after an invoice was sent does the same in either
  direction.
- Fix: cancel and edit should reverse what was actually moved for that invoice (sum of its `sale` and `return`
  movements), not its current lines.

**S3 · High · A delivery note can't be made by hand** · Verified in code
- Where: `DeliveryController.php:460` (transaction) then `:481` (`generate_doc_number` opens another);
  `:361` searches `invoices.deleted_at`, which doesn't exist (500).
- Fix: transaction-aware doc numbers (as M4); drop the bad column; one live delivery per invoice.

**S4 · High · Cancelling an approved order leaves its invoice, stock and delivery live** · Verified in code
- Where: `OrderController.php:361-385`; `InvoiceController.php:477-558`.
- What happens: the invoice still counts as money owed and later raises an overdue debt; stock stays off; the
  delivery can still be signed and asks for a review. Cancelling the invoice instead leaves the order
  "approved", so the shopper's tracking page still says it's being prepared.
- Fix: cancelling either one cancels the other, closes the delivery and refuses sign-off.

**S5 · Medium · Editing an invoice doesn't update its delivery note or order** (`InvoiceController.php:826`):
the customer signs for the old quantities. Fix: rebuild unsigned delivery lines, or block the edit.

**S6 · Medium · Two stock changes at once can overwrite each other** · Verified in code
- Where: `InvoiceController.php:297-314,496-512`; `ReceiptController.php:426-436`; `ProductController.php:422-436`
  read then write an absolute count without a lock.
- Fix: lock the row (`FOR UPDATE`) as the newer paths do.

**S7 · Medium · Booking confirm and decline race** (`BookingController.php:98-112,228-233`): two confirms add an
orphan appointment that blocks a slot; confirm plus decline leaves a declined booking on the calendar. Fix:
guarded status updates.

**S8 · Medium · Anyone can fill a shop's booking calendar** (`StorefrontController.php:453-457`): pending
bookings hold their time forever; the only limit is 5 a day per typed phone number. Fix: throttle by IP and let
unconfirmed bookings expire.

**S9 · Medium · Changing goods to a service sets stock to 0 without a record** (`ProductController.php:277-295`).

**S10 · Medium · Checkout accepts products the shop doesn't sell, bookable services and out-of-stock goods**
(`PublicController.php:749-751`). Prices are safe (they come from the database).

**S11 · Medium · Sign-off ignores the delivery's state and can save twice** (`PublicController.php:330-336,378-381`).

**S12 · Low · Manual stock adjustment edge cases** (`ProductController.php:411,427,439`): can't set 0; a
stock-out larger than the stock logs the wrong number; a negative count jumps to 0.

**S13 · Low · Product import and edit accept negative stock and prices** (`ProductController.php:622-644,257-259`).

**S14 · Low · A fixed deposit disappears for services priced 0** (`bookings.php:99,134`).

**S15 · Low · Invoice lines and deliveries can point at another business's product, invoice or customer**
(`InvoiceController.php:772`, `DeliveryController.php:410-411,512`). Part of the M1 fix.

**S16 · Low · A deleted delivery still drives the shopper's tracking page** (`shop_orders.php:58,170`).

**S17 · Low · Approving can attach the invoice to a deleted customer** (`OrderController.php:409-410`); it should
use the order's own `customer_id`.

**S18 · Low · "Need restocking" counts differ between Home and the Stock page** (`ProductController.php:62-74,112-121`).

## X · Security

Each business's records are scoped by `business_id` on reads by id, and child rows are reached through a
checked parent. The gaps are the posted ids in M1/S15, missing role checks, and a few unguarded actions.

**X1 · Critical · Any successful Paystack reference can be replayed to get a paid month** · Verified in code
- Where: `PaystackController.php:123-183` (callback), `:150` (credits the signed-in business), `:490-513`
  (the duplicate guard runs after activation and swallows the error).
- What happens: open `/billing/callback?reference=<any successful reference>`, your own old one or another
  business's, and your business is activated for another month, as often as you like. The webhook has the
  duplicate check; the callback doesn't. The amount and currency aren't checked either.
- Fix: in the callback, claim the reference first (insert, stop on duplicate), require
  `metadata.business_id` to match, check amount and currency; or let only the webhook activate.

**X2 · High · Staff can open owner-only pages** · Verified in code
- Where: constructors of `ExpenseController.php:34-38`, `DebtController.php:18-22`, `ReportController.php:13-17`,
  `OrderController.php:12-19`, `PostController.php:19-26`, `BookingController.php:21-27` only check sign-in;
  the menu (`nav.php:72-99`) is the only barrier.
- What happens: a staff member types `/reports/profit-loss`, `/expenses` or `/debts` and reads the business's
  finances, adds or deletes expenses and debts, approves orders, posts news.
- Fix: the same role rule as the menu, checked in each controller.

**X3 · High · Anyone in business 1 is a platform admin** · Verified in code
- Where: `AdminController.php:19-26`, `CountryController.php:19-27`, `MoMoController.php:498-504`, `nav.php:101-107`.
- What happens: a staff member added to the founder's business can activate or suspend any tenant, approve
  Mobile Money payments, change country prices, hide reviews.
- Fix: require the owner role (or a platform-admin flag) as well.

**X4 · Medium · Script injection through a delivery's "provider"** · Verified in code
- Where: `DeliveryController.php:416,475,500` (stored as posted); `views/deliveries/public.php:178`,
  `view.php:207`, `index.php:110` print an unknown provider unescaped.
- What happens: a crafted provider value runs script on the public delivery and sign-off pages the customer
  opens, and in the business's own screens.
- Fix: accept only the listed providers; escape the fallback.

**X5 · Medium · Purge doesn't check which table it deletes from** (`PurgeController.php:238`, used at
:393,411,529,619). Owner + PIN only, still scoped by business, but the table name comes from the request.

**X6 · Medium · Public quote actions have no state guard, replay protection or rate limit**
(`PublicController.php:67-184`). See M8.

**X7 · Medium · Signing out works by GET**, so another site can sign a user out (`index.php:307-310`).

**X8 · Low · `/api/set-country` changes the business's country and currency with no CSRF or role check**
(`api/ajax/set_country.php`). Part of B3.

**X9 · Low · `uploads/` has no rule against running scripts** (defence in depth; nothing writes scripts there today).

**X10 · Low · Public document links never expire** (by design; noted for later).

## B · Billing, trials and staff seats

**B1 · Critical · Paid accounts never expire** · Verified in code
- Where: `subscription_check.php:62` ("'active' falls through"); `shop_links.php:310-311`; `planner.php:831-832`.
- What happens: only Paystack's own renewal events ever move an account out of `active`. A one-off payment
  (any plan with extra seats), an approved Mobile Money receipt, an admin activation or a replayed callback
  (X1) gives access, a live shop and reminders forever.
- Fix: treat `active` past `current_period_end` (+3 days grace) as past due everywhere access is decided.

**B2 · Critical · A Mobile Money "receipt" upload gives full access immediately, repeatably** · Verified in code
- Where: `MoMoController.php:79-174` (status set to `pending_verification` and seats granted before review);
  `subscription_check.php:46-50` (no time limit); `api/ajax/set_country.php` (any user can switch to a MoMo
  country).
- What happens: switch to a Mobile Money country, upload any picture, and the business is unlocked with up to
  4 extra seats until an admin rejects it, then upload again.
- Fix: keep the old status while a receipt waits (at most one 72-hour grace per period); grant seats only on
  approval; owner-only country changes; rate-limit submissions.

**B3 · High · Rejecting a Mobile Money receipt gives an endless trial** (`MoMoController.php:410-418`): the business
returns to `trial` with no end date. Fix: restore the previous status, period and seats.

**B4 · High · Paying for fewer seats than active staff** (`PaystackController.php:70,81,385`;
`MoMoController.php:95,171`): renewing with 0 extra seats leaves 4 staff working. Fix: refuse a seat count below
active staff, or pause the extra staff.

**B5 · High · Seats added mid-cycle are charged once, then never again** (`PaystackController.php:72-78,85`).

**B6 · High · Webhook duplicate keys collide** (`PaystackController.php:229-241`): cancellations and payment
failures can be dropped as "already processed". Reviewed; depends on Paystack's payload.

**B7 · Medium · Cancelling cuts access the same day**, though the page promises access to the end of the period.

**B8 · Medium · Cancel and admin suspend don't reliably stop Paystack**, and the next charge re-activates.

**B9 · Medium · Re-subscribing while past due can create a second Paystack subscription** (double billing). Reviewed.

**B10 · Medium · Referral credit is used up without being applied, and can be used twice** (`PaystackController.php:83-96,167-169,343-346`).

**B11 · Medium · A business's referral code changes on every visit** (`ReferralController.php:29-34`), so shared
codes stop working; `create_link` can insert a NULL code (500).

**B12 · Medium · Country prices and currency don't match what Paystack charges** (fixed NGN amounts in
`PaystackController.php:19-20,77,81,93` vs `countries.monthly_price`).

**B13 · Medium · Expired trial + unfinished onboarding = endless redirect, can't pay**
(`auth_check.php:128-129` vs `subscription_check.php:12`).

**B14 · Medium · Removed staff keep working until they sit idle 2 hours**, and their seat is free at once
(`SettingsController.php:512`).

**B15 · Medium · Mobile Money receipts can be reused and approved twice**; paying early throws away the rest of
the period (`MoMoController.php:177-186,308-317`).

**B16 · Low · Admin "extend trial" can't revive an expired trial; admin actions aren't logged** (`AdminController.php:178-195`).

**B17 · Low · Admin MRR and revenue chart use the wrong units** (kobo shown as naira; XAF added as naira).

**B18 · Low · The webhook accepts anything if the Paystack key is missing** (`PaystackController.php:24,202`).

**B19 · Low · Unlimited new trials by signing up again**; `countries.trial_days` is ignored (`AuthController.php:139`).

**B20 · Low · Date arithmetic**: `+1 month` from 31 January is 3 March; trials end at midnight at the start of
the last day.

**B21 · Low · A removed staff member's email can't be added again**, and that person can't register.

**B22 · Low · Dead ends**: "manual" countries are sent to a billing panel that doesn't exist; suspended owners

**B23 · Low · Staff can start payments and change the country; `update_role` has no CSRF check; the ₦ in
payment notices shows as "â¦"** (`PaystackController.php:176,355`).

## W · Shop websites

Owner and shopper text is escaped everywhere on the shop pages; no script injection was found there. Prices
always come from the database. The gaps are abuse limits, uploads and a few broken promises.

**W1 · High · Anyone can fill the server's disk through payment-proof uploads** · Verified in code
- Where: `StorefrontController.php:453-483` (booking proof saved before the slot check, never deleted on
  failure), `PublicController.php:798-814` (order proof), `functions.php:188` (`save_image` only limits width).
- What happens: a script posts bookings for a taken slot with a huge image; each request leaves a file
  behind. A tall image can be tens of MB. On shared hosting a full disk takes every shop and the app down.
- Fix: size and pixel limits; save the proof only after the order or booking is committed (or delete it on
  failure); throttle by IP (using Cloudflare's `CF-Connecting-IP`).

**W2 · High · No real spam limit on checkout, quote requests and bookings** · Reviewed
- Where: `PublicController.php:633-645,785-797,926-950`; `StorefrontController.php:527-539`. Limits key on the
  phone number as typed.

**Three root causes behind many findings:**

## Decisions (Tom, 18 October 2026)

## Method and limits

- Seven reviews ran in parallel, one per area, reading the code end to end against the production schema
  (`db/schema.sql` plus migrations).
- The findings with the most at stake were then re-checked by hand. M1 was reproduced in the running app, M3
  in PHP, and the rest of the Critical and High findings were traced in the code.
- Not covered:
  - load or performance testing;
  - Paystack's live behaviour, which was taken from its documented payloads;
  - the production stored procedure's exact number format (D5);
  - browser testing beyond the flows named above.
