# The tutorial walkthrough

October 2026. Before filming five tutorials for a hair vendor (Adanna Luxe
Hair, a made-up business), we walked the whole app as she would: signing up
on a phone, onboarding, adding products, opening the shop, taking orders and
payments, delivering, quoting, keeping customers' money, recording expenses,
reading the reports and records, and adding staff. Everything found was
fixed, ruled out, or put to Tom and then built.

## What Tom decided

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Tax on a new business | Starts at 0%, with "Add your VAT rate if you charge it" |
| Staff on the free trial | The trial includes one staff member |
| What Profit & Loss counts | Cash in and cash out; stock bought shows as its own line, "Spent on stock" |
| Sold-out items on the shop | A shop setting, on for new shops and off for existing ones |
| Changing the category | Settings › Business; the shop's look stays as it is |
| Currency | Follows the country; shown read-only in onboarding and Settings |
| A shared draft quote | Becomes Sent when its link is shared |
| A quote waiting to sign | The owner can edit it, for a new date |

## Improvements found while filming

- The onboarding wizard now looks like sign-up, and the item form puts the
  photos first and speaks plainly. Hair vendors get a Hair & Beauty category,
  bundles as a unit, and plural counts ("12 bundles").
- Customers no longer meet a browser pop-up: accepting a quote asks in the
  page, and the shop's checkout has a proper screenshot picker.
- Every screen the tutorials show is in sentence case, at readable sizes.

## Still open

- The owner's own delete and status confirmations still use the browser's box
  (listed in `4-quotes-deliveries.md`, under Round 4).
- "Profit on what you sold" (sales minus the cost of the items sold) is for
  later, once cost prices are reliably filled in.
