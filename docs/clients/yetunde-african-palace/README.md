## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Name | Yetunde African Palace, never translated, no accents. Short form: Yetunde |
| Lead name | The restaurant name leads; ETS TANKET is the legal name on documents and the stamp |
| Personality | Royal hospitality: proud, generous, warm, never stiff |
| Her role in the brand | Parked until after the project (leaning: her story, the house's name) |
| Colours | Forest green #1F4D3A, gold #C9A24B, palm red #B23A21, cream #F7F1E6, cocoa #2B1E17 |
| Symbol | A cooking pot whose steam rises into a crown |
| Type | Fraunces (name, headings), DM Sans (everything else) |
| Local look | Modern, one signature: the royal band (crowns and diamonds, after adire and ndop cloth) |
| Languages | Documents: one language each, picked on the document. Everything else: French first, same size |
| Tagline | Ici, chaque invité est roi. / Here, every guest is royalty. |
| Voice | Warm host, « tu » (never « vous »); local touches that work in both languages; Pidgin and Camfranglais on social only; documents stay neutral |
| Photos | AI-generated in one house style (the koki reference), approved by her, replaced by real photos over time |

Palm red stays the appetite colour on print, packaging and social. In the app it
only marks errors, so the Commander / Order button is forest green. Pending
states use deep gold #7E6020; gold never sets small text on light backgrounds.

## Logo prompt (ChatGPT Images 2.5)

```
Design a logo for "Yetunde African Palace", a restaurant and catering house in Bertoua, Cameroon, serving traditional African dishes. The brand idea is royal hospitality: every guest is treated like royalty. The logo must feel warm, proud and premium, like a seal on a palace door, never like fast food.

SYMBOL
A traditional round cooking pot seen straight from the front, perfectly symmetrical: a wide rounded belly, a short neck, a slightly flared rim and two small loop handles on its sides. Three wisps of steam rise from the mouth of the pot and end in the three points of a crown: the middle wisp is the tallest, the outer two curl gently outward. The rim of the pot doubles as the band of the crown. One plain band runs around the belly of the pot.

LETTERING
To the right of the symbol, two lines, left-aligned:
1. "Yetunde": large, in a warm soft serif with gently rounded serifs, in the spirit of Fraunces SemiBold.
2. "AFRICAN PALACE": much smaller, a clean geometric sans-serif in the spirit of DM Sans Medium, all capitals, widely letter-spaced, as wide as "Yetunde".
Spell it exactly: Yetunde (Y-e-t-u-n-d-e, no accents), African Palace. No other words anywhere.

COLOURS (flat, exact, nothing else)
Forest green #1F4D3A: the pot, its handles and both lines of lettering.
Warm gold #C9A24B: the steam crown only.
Cream #F7F1E6: the background, and the band on the pot's belly (it reads as a gap in the green).

STYLE
Flat vector logo with solid filled shapes and crisp edges. No outlines, gradients, shadows, glow, 3D or texture. Simple and bold enough to read at 48 pixels and to work as a one-colour rubber stamp. The symbol is as tall as the two lines of lettering together.

LAYOUT
Horizontal logo centred on a 3:2 canvas with generous empty margins on every side.

AVOID
Forks, knives, spoons, chef hats, fire under the pot, flags, maps of Africa, people or faces, domes or minarets, cartoon or clip-art looks, script or handwritten fonts, taglines, frames, mockups, background scenes.

Make three versions as separate images that differ only in how the pot and the steam crown are drawn. Keep the lettering and colours identical.
```

Follow-ups in the same chat, once one is picked:

- Stacked: `Same logo, stacked: the symbol on top, "Yetunde" centred below it, "AFRICAN PALACE" centred below that. Same colours and proportions, square 1:1 canvas, generous margins.`
- App icon: `Only the symbol, no lettering, on a solid forest green #1F4D3A square: pot in cream #F7F1E6, steam crown in light gold #DDBE72, belly band in forest green. Keep it inside the middle 70% of the square. 1:1.`
- On green: `The horizontal logo on a solid forest green #1F4D3A background: pot and lettering in cream #F7F1E6, steam crown in light gold #DDBE72. 3:2.`
- Stamp: `The horizontal logo in forest green #1F4D3A only, on white, no gold, as it would print from a rubber stamp. 3:2.`

The chosen image gets redrawn as a clean SVG; the app icons, favicon, seal and
stamp are cut from that, not from the PNG.

## Dish-photo prompt

```
Professional food photograph of [DISH], a traditional Cameroonian dish, exactly as served at a family restaurant in Bertoua: [WHAT IS ON THE PLATE]. On a speckled cream stoneware plate on a round woven raffia mat, on a warm dark wooden table. A forest-green linen napkin at the edge of the frame; a clay pot and woven baskets softly blurred behind. Soft natural daylight from the left, gentle shadows, a light wisp of steam. Three-quarter view at 45 degrees, shallow depth of field, realistic portion, true colours. No people, no hands, no text, no logos. Square 1:1.
```

Fill-ins for `[WHAT IS ON THE PLATE]`. Check each against her menu: portion,
sides and dish must match what her kitchen serves.

- Ndolé: dark green stew of bitter leaves and ground groundnuts with beef and shrimp, a few whole shrimp on top, fried ripe plantain on the side
- Eru: finely shredded dark green eru leaves cooked with waterleaf, red palm oil, smoked fish, cow skin and crayfish, glossy, with a smooth white ball of water fufu
- Koki: golden-orange steamed black-eyed-pea cake with red palm oil and leaves, half unwrapped from its banana leaf, with boiled plantain
- Poulet DG: pieces of fried chicken tossed with fried ripe plantain, carrots, green beans and sweet peppers in a light tomato sauce
- Poisson braisé: a whole grilled fish with charred skin, topped with a spicy green herb and pepper sauce, sliced onions and tomato, with miondo on the side
- Beignets haricots: golden puff-puff, red beans in a light sauce, and a bowl of pap
- Breakfast package: an omelette, sliced bread, a cup of tea and puff-puff (the 2 000 and 3 000 FCFA packages from the call)

For a platter or package, switch the angle to `Top-down view` and the plate to
`a round woven tray`.
