# UX profile: Nfelia

Read by the UI/UX skills before layout, colour, type or motion changes.

## Audience
Nigerian informal-sector business owners: market traders and shop owners,
Instagram/WhatsApp sellers, service businesses (events, beauty, fashion).
Mostly on phones, often on paid mobile data. Short sentences, everyday
Nigerian English; no enterprise jargon ("protocol", "architecture",
"deploy", "SKU", "CRM").

## Brand
- Burgundy `#8B004A` (primary), deep `#5E0032`, tint `#F8EEF3`
- Gold `#B69F5E`: decorative only on white (low contrast); fine on dark
- Ink `#1F0A14`, secondary text `#5B4650`
- Money in: green `#00764A`. Money owed: red `#B42318`
- Public pages: Bricolage Grotesque (headlines), Plus Jakarta Sans (body)
- Motif: the thread that holds the business together (burgundy line,
  gold stitches)

## Banned
Rainbow or multi-hue gradients on buttons, glassmorphism, cartoon people,
all-caps eyebrow labels, monospace data labels as decoration, invented
testimonials or customer numbers.

## Notes
- Marketing pages outside the app shell (views/home, views/auth) may use
  one longer hero sequence: the thread draws over 1.6s on first load, and
  the feature seam is drawn with scroll (CSS scroll-driven animation, static
  where unsupported). Everything else stays on the 100/150/200/300ms scale,
  and all of it is off under prefers-reduced-motion.
- App screens follow the standard motion scale. The one exception is the
  installed app's opening (views/partials/nf_splash.php, assets/js/splash.js),
  chosen by the owner: the brand film on the first launch after install,
  then a gold thread of under 2 seconds. A tap skips it, it never runs past
  9 seconds, and under prefers-reduced-motion it is a short fade.
- No frosted glass: the app bar and tabs are solid colour.
