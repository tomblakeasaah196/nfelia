# Working on Nfelia with Claude

## Asking me questions

When you need decisions from me, ask with the AskUserQuestion tool, not as a list in chat.

- Up to 4 questions per call. Use as many calls as the topic needs; 20 questions over 5 calls is fine.
- Give each question 2 to 4 concrete options, each with a one-line trade-off.
- Put your recommendation first, add "(Recommended)" to its label, and say why in its description.
- Use multi-select only when the options can be combined.
- Check what you can reach yourself first (the code, the hosting, Cloudflare) and only ask what is genuinely my call.
